Who We Are

ESTHADAM GLOBAL INVESTMENTS AND SERVICES is a dynamic manufacturing company based in Ofu Local Government Area, Kogi State. We are committed to delivering world-class manufacturing solutions that meet the evolving needs of Nigerian businesses and industries.

With a strategic location in Emewe Ochadamu, we serve clients across Kogi State and throughout Nigeria, providing comprehensive manufacturing services backed by technical expertise and modern equipment. Our team combines local knowledge with international standards to ensure every project meets the highest quality benchmarks.

We believe in building lasting partnerships with our clients, understanding their unique challenges, and delivering customized solutions that create real value. From concept to completion, ESTHADAM GLOBAL is your reliable partner for manufacturing excellence.
